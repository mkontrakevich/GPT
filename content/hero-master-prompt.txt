# CSUM Nizhny Novgorod — CANONICAL HERO MASTER PROMPT

PURPOSE.
Create the single canonical HERO image for the CSUM Nizhny Novgorod digital magazine. This is the master visual, not a weather variation.

REAL SUBJECT.
The subject is the HISTORIC OLD FACADE of the real CSUM Nizhny Novgorod building at 10 Geroya Filchenkova Street / Revolution Square. The supplied verified real photograph is factual ground truth.

SOURCE-FIRST / IMAGE-TO-IMAGE ONLY.
Never invent the building from text. Do not replace it with a generic department store. Do not modernize, redesign or beautify architecture by changing its facts.

HISTORIC FACADE LOCK.
Preserve exactly:
- building massing and proportions;
- floor count;
- facade geometry;
- rhythm, size and position of windows;
- columns and pilasters;
- entrances;
- cornices and roofline;
- characteristic historic details.
The building must remain immediately recognizable as the real historic CSUM.

FRONTAL ORTHOGRAPHIC HERO LOCK.
The final HERO must read as a beautiful frontal architectural portrait:
- camera optical axis approximately perpendicular to the principal historic facade;
- near-orthographic appearance;
- verticals strictly vertical;
- horizontals level;
- minimal keystone distortion;
- minimal wide-angle exaggeration;
- no dramatic three-quarter or diagonal architectural angle;
- no fake symmetry created by changing architecture.
If the source is slightly oblique, correct perspective conservatively while preserving factual geometry.

FACADE PRIORITY.
The historic facade is the undisputed protagonist. Frame it cleanly and confidently, with enough surrounding city context to remain believable but without visual competition.

REMOVE OBSTRUCTIVE ENGINEERING CLUTTER.
Remove or naturally minimize objects only when they materially cut across or hide the facade:
- lighting poles and lamp posts;
- overhead electrical or trolley wires;
- utility cables;
- technical masts;
- temporary barriers and miscellaneous street hardware.
Reconstruct only newly revealed facade portions from verified architecture. Do not erase necessary city context simply to make the scene empty.

CITYSCAPE LOCK.
Keep all recognizable surrounding buildings, street geometry and urban relationships geographically credible. Never invent skyline, neighboring architecture or landmarks. If uncertain, crop or simplify rather than hallucinate.

CANONICAL EDITORIAL GRADE.
This master HERO establishes the visual grade for the entire site:
- high-end international architecture/fashion/culture editorial photography;
- neutral-to-warm natural daylight;
- soft controlled highlights;
- rich but open shadows;
- restrained saturation;
- natural stone, plaster, glass and asphalt tones;
- subtle filmic tonal roll-off;
- tactile materials;
- realistic human scale;
- calm premium composition.
The image must feel photographed, not rendered.

CAMERA LANGUAGE.
Premium large-format architectural editorial character, approximately 45–60 mm full-frame equivalent after perspective correction. Natural detail, no artificial shallow depth of field on the building.

FORBIDDEN LOOKS.
No CGI gloss.
No HDR.
No over-sharpening.
No plastic surfaces.
No dramatic sunset/golden-hour spectacle.
No theatrical storm light.
No luxury-ad artificiality.
No fantasy city.
No generic AI cleanliness.
No poster-like typography inside the image.

BRAND / TYPOGRAPHY LOCK.
Do not generate, redraw, reinterpret or hallucinate logos, store names or signage. Verified branding belongs to separate original assets or HTML/composited layers.

BASE WEATHER.
Do NOT use live weather for the canonical HERO. Use timeless, believable neutral daylight with light atmospheric depth. Weather variants may only be derived later from this approved master.

FINAL QA — REJECT IF:
- facade is not predominantly frontal;
- historic facade identity changes;
- floor/window/column rhythm changes;
- entrance or roofline is invented;
- engineering clutter still materially blocks the facade where natural cleanup is possible;
- recognizable city context is fabricated;
- generated signage or malformed text appears;
- lighting becomes dramatic advertising imagery;
- image reads as CGI/render rather than editorial photography.

TARGET RESULT.
A timeless, elegant, frontal, near-orthographic editorial portrait of the REAL HISTORIC CSUM Nizhny Novgorod facade: immediately recognizable, cleanly readable, naturally beautiful and suitable as the permanent visual master of the magazine.
