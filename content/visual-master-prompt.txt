SOURCE-FIRST / IMAGE-TO-IMAGE ONLY.
Every published site image must be generated from a verified real reference image. The source image is factual ground truth for the place, object, architecture, material relationships and recognizable details. Never substitute a generic invented scene when a real source exists.

EDITORIAL QUALITY.
Create a publication-grade photograph for a high-end international architecture, fashion and culture magazine: observational, credible, tactile and naturally art-directed. Realistic available or motivated light, restrained filmic grading, natural skin/material texture, subtle imperfections, physically plausible depth of field, 35mm/50mm full-frame photographic character.

NO AI ADVERTISING LOOK.

FACT LOCK.
Preserve factual attributes visible in the verified source. Do not invent architecture, landmarks, products, logos, storefront identities or city geometry. If a detail cannot be verified, simplify or crop rather than hallucinate.

BRAND LOCK.
Do not redraw logos or branded typography. Verified brand marks belong to original assets or separate composited/HTML layers.

ARCHITECTURE MODE.

HERO ORTHO MODE.

For hero-csum, the facade is the clear protagonist.
